# Label

Field label. On its own it is 14px medium — but the house pattern is the **micro
label**: 11px, semibold, uppercase, wide tracking, muted colour, sitting directly
above its control. That stack is the system's main hierarchy device and appears
throughout the app.

It renders as a flex row with an 8px gap, so a trailing hint ("nepovinné") can sit
inline after the text. It dims automatically when its peer or group is disabled.
